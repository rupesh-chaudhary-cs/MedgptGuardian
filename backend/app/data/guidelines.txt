Clinical Protocol: Penicillin Cross-Reactivity Guidelines 2024
Patients with severe penicillin allergies should strictly avoid Amoxicillin due to high cross-reactivity. Alternative treatments include Macrolides (e.g., Azithromycin) or Doxycycline.

Standard Care Guidelines for Viral Infections
Standard care for viral sinus infections includes rest, hydration, and over-the-counter paracetamol. Antibiotics are not recommended unless bacterial infection is confirmed.
